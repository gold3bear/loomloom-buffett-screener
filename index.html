FOUR LENSES, ONE EVIDENCE DEFINITION

We don't let the model "pick stocks." Each filter runs its own evidence audit, and a hard local veto gates the ranking.

The framework emphasizes understanding the business model, long-term cash flow, moat, management, and capital allocation. Price judgment must bind to both a price timestamp and a fiscal period; without that anchor, the row is flagged evidence_thin in the report instead of being silently approved.
